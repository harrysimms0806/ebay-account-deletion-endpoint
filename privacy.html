Privacy Policy

Harry's eBay Selling Helper is a private application operated by Harry for managing his own eBay selling activity. It is not offered to the public.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Who is responsible

Harry, the owner and operator of this personal application, is responsible for information handled by it. For privacy enquiries, contact Harry through the contact details associated with his eBay account or the account through which this application was provided.

Information accessed

If Harry connects an eBay account, the application may access only the information permitted by the eBay authorisation and needed for the selected selling features. Depending on features enabled, this may include eBay account identifiers, inventory and listing details, order and fulfilment details, and buyer information contained in orders.

The application is intended to use this information solely to help Harry prepare, review, and manage his own listings and sales. It is not used to build advertising profiles, sell personal information, or make information available to other users.

How information is handled

Information is processed by the application and may pass through eBay and the infrastructure providers used to host or operate it (currently Railway and related infrastructure). Those providers process information to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy notices. The application does not intentionally disclose eBay information to unrelated third parties, except where required by law or necessary to operate the service.

The application is being configured to avoid storing eBay data unless a feature genuinely requires it. Any information that is stored will be limited to that purpose, protected with reasonable access controls, and kept only for as long as needed for the relevant selling workflow or legal obligations. Do not rely on this statement as a promise that no data is stored: actual retention depends on the features and hosting configuration in use.

Security and credentials

eBay passwords are not requested by this application. Access is granted through eBay's authorisation flow and can be revoked by Harry in eBay account settings. Application credentials and access tokens should be held in protected configuration, not public source code or chat. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

When information is no longer needed for the stated purpose, it should be deleted or securely removed, subject to applicable legal requirements. If the application receives an eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notification, it acknowledges the notification; any eBay user data associated with a deletion request must be located and deleted from systems under the operator's control in accordance with eBay requirements. The notification endpoint itself is designed not to retain notification request bodies.

Harry can disconnect the eBay account or revoke the application's access through eBay. For a privacy request concerning this tool, contact the operator using the eBay account contact route described above.

Children

This personal tool is not directed to children and is not intended for their use.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the application's data practices change. The current version is published at this URL.

More information

eBay's own privacy practices are described in the eBay Privacy Notice. This policy covers only Harry's separate personal application.
