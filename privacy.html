Privacy Policy

Harry's eBay Selling Helper is a private tool used by Harry to organise and manage his own eBay selling activity.

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Information handled

If connected to eBay, the tool may access the eBay account information and data needed for the features Harry chooses to use, such as inventory, listings, and order information. Data is used only to support Harry's own selling activity.

Storage and sharing

The tool is intended for Harry's personal use, not for public users. It does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information is not intentionally shared with other people, except with eBay and service providers necessary to operate the tool (such as its hosting provider), or where required by law.

Operational data should be kept only as long as needed for the chosen workflow. Any eBay user data retained by the tool will be handled in accordance with applicable eBay requirements, including deletion requests. The account-deletion notification service is designed to acknowledge notifications without storing their contents.

Security

Access credentials and tokens should be stored in protected service configuration, not in source code or public messages. No internet-hosted service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and contact

Harry controls whether to connect the account and can revoke application access through eBay. For privacy questions or requests about this personal tool, contact the account owner through the contact method associated with the eBay account.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the tool's operation or data handling changes. The current version will be published at this page.
